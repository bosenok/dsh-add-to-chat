## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added

- Annotation chips and cards that attach quoted text and comments to the
  outgoing message.
- Send-control detection for labelled and unlabelled composers.

### Changed

- The send control is matched by its English label only, and the documentation
  is English.
- Packaged as a DSH bundle.
